08 / YOUR NEXT LEVEL

Your level planner

STATIC ESTIMATE

Plan from your current level to a level, league or feature. Your account settings stay in this browser; history filters do not change this plan.

Your starting point

Enter your current level once to save it as your default. The payments export does not contain account XP.

Available tokens start from the full saved history’s net movement, not a live balance. You can correct the value. Exact XP improves estimates; otherwise the plan starts at your level’s minimum XP.

XP helpers and bonuses

Imported assumptions: 5 XP per token spent; 1 watching XP per 15 minutes, capped at level 9; email bonus below level 10. The first-purchase bonus applies only when crossing into level 10 and requires a purchase. Enable only bonuses you have not already received.

Explore a level

Reference thresholds only; this does not estimate your account XP.

Leagues, unlocks and package reference Imported calculator assumptions

Community-reported XP thresholds and imported unlock rules may differ from current Stripchat rules. Package planning uses the original calculator’s six EUR packages: as many full 2,250-token packs as possible, then the cheapest smaller-pack combination for the remainder. This policy is not a globally cheapest package search. No purchase is made.
